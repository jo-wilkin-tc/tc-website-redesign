How we work

From hidden data to community change

Uncover

We make the invisible visible.

We build data infrastructure, curate novel datasets, and conduct interdisciplinary research to reveal patterns and insights hidden in — or ignored by — standard systems.

Translate

We make evidence accessible and actionable.

We communicate complex data and science in ways that are relevant and meaningful, building the knowledge and capacity communities need to protect and advocate for their wellbeing.

Partner

We co-create with communities.

We work alongside Tribal nations, rural, urban, and farmworker communities, and environmental justice organizations as co-investigators to build effective solutions to local challenges.

The outcome of our work together

Transform

Communities that are safer, better informed, and better equipped to advocate for their health — and data and science that shift what decision-makers pay attention to.

Where we're focused

Our priority areas

The environmental health challenges where our data, research, and partnerships can do the most for California's most affected communities.

Air quality

Community monitoring networks, PM2.5 and smoke exposure surveillance, and technical assistance for AB 617 communities.

Climate resilience

Community-driven planning and local capacity building to prepare rural and frontline communities for a changing climate.

Extreme heat

Heat-related illness surveillance, impacts on vulnerable populations, and heat-action planning and resilience.

Pesticides

Mapping agricultural pesticide use and proximity to homes, schools, and pregnant residents across California.

Sickle cell disease

Disease surveillance and health services research to improve care and outcomes for people living with sickle cell disease.

Water quality

Drinking water surveillance, exposure assessment for PFAS and harmful algal blooms, and support for small water systems.

Alongside these, we sustain deep work in environmental epidemiology, health services research, and emerging hazards such as lithium extraction.
