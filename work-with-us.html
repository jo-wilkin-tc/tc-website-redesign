Work with us

A research partner communities trust

For over two decades, Tracking California has paired rigorous environmental health data and research with deep, lasting relationships in the communities most affected. Funders and partners come to us for both.

State agencies and academic partners rely on our rigor and credibility. Tribal Nations, farmworker communities, and environmental justice organizations rely on our long-term commitment and our willingness to show up, stay, and work on what matters to them. They come to us for data and science, content expertise, and thought leadership — and they stay because we make visible what standard monitoring systems are not designed to detect: the conditions, exposures, and health outcomes that fall through the cracks.

Why partner with us

What communities, funders, and collaborators gain by working with Tracking California.

  • Deep community partnerships. Long-term, co-investigator relationships with Tribal Nations, farmworker, rural, and environmental justice communities — not parachute engagements.
  • Data and research know-how. Environmental health data systems, methods, and institutional knowledge built and maintained since the early 2000s.
  • Dense networks across public health. Established connections to community organizations, public agencies, and researchers working on environmental health throughout California.
  • An agency and academic track record. We co-develop the state's environmental health tracking data and publish peer-reviewed research.

What makes a good fit

We're selective by design, so that the work we take on is work we can do well. The strongest opportunities tend to:

Build on our strengths

Draw on existing relationships, data infrastructure, and expertise rather than starting from scratch.

Center data and research

Surveillance, analysis, and infrastructure — the work that reveals what standard systems miss.

Serve California communities

Especially the rural, Tribal, farmworker, and environmental justice communities we partner with.

Create lasting impact

A clear difference for communities, for knowledge, or for our own capacity.
